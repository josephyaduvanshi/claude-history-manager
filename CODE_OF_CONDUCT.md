# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Don't harass, don't dismiss, don't punch down. Disagreement is fine; cruelty isn't.

## What we expect

- Treat people respectfully, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.
- When you disagree, focus on the idea or the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume good faith first. People file vague bug reports because they're frustrated, not because they want to waste your time.
- If someone asks you to stop a behavior that's making them uncomfortable, stop.
- If you're a maintainer, your reach matters more. Hold yourself to a higher bar in public threads.

## What we won't tolerate

- Personal attacks, slurs, or harassment in any form.
- Sexualized comments, imagery, or attention.
- Doxxing, threats, or sharing private information without consent.
- Sustained derailment of issues, PRs, or discussions.
- Repeatedly arguing in bad faith after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If you see or experience any of the above, email josefyaduvanshi@gmail.com with `[chronicle-conduct]` in the subject. I'll respond within 7 days. Reports stay confidential. I'll act in proportion to the issue: a private nudge for a single rough comment, removal from the repo for a pattern.

If the issue involves me directly, that's still the right address. Be specific about what happened so I can take it seriously.

## Scope

This applies to GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, and any spaces where people are talking about Chronicle as a project (including in-person events someone shows up to representing the project). It doesn't reach into people's private lives or unrelated communities.

Loosely modeled on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), but rewritten to read like a person actually wrote it.
